# Release scope decision — 2 September 2026

The researched brief records a future freemium opportunity: a one-time Circle
Plus license with organization controls and additional storage. This release
does not implement that tier.

The shipped product is deliberately limited to one adult-owned private math
circle per deployment. It has no paid plan, checkout, organization controls,
or extra storage tier. There is no hidden checkout, license form, purchase
copy, or dead external payment link. The four reusable strategy prompts are
included free in every board instead.

This is an explicit product-scope deviation, not a claim that an unavailable
billing flow exists. It keeps the researched core job intact—planning and
recording a tiny private math-circle session—while avoiding an untestable
organization purchase path. A future tier needs a registered Sociobot product,
concrete organization data model and controls, an observable purchase test,
and a revised claims manifest before it can be advertised.
